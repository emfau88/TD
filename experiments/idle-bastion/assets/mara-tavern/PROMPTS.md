These must be coherent successive animation drawings with intermediate arm and head poses, not twelve unrelated illustrations. Preserve exact identity, belt position, lower body, outfit details and physical scale throughout. No floating hearts, no effects, no whole-body translations, no extra characters. Only 12 complete frames in the stated 4x3 grid.
