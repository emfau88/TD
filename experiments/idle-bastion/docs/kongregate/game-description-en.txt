Towers & Tantrums
Build towers. Make questionable friends.

A quiet forest settlement. A growing army of pests. Companions with opinions.

Build and upgrade your defenses, combine slowing frost with hard-hitting towers, and keep the core standing through advancing enemy waves. Combat runs automatically while you choose what to build, research new options and put your companions' abilities to work.

Between battles, visit The Last Cozy Corner. Get to know Mara and Ilyra through conversations, gifts and shared moments. Apparently saving the grove also requires social skills.

Features:
- Tower defense with idle progression and offline gold.
- Different tower roles, research and companion abilities.
- A growing settlement and a tavern with character stories.
- Mouse and touch controls; English and German interface and text. Character voices are in English.

This version includes the first three relationship hearts for each companion. Further relationship content is planned for future updates.

Intended for ages 13 and up. Contains fantasy combat and mild flirtatious dialogue. This is the developer's voluntary age recommendation.

AI disclosure: AI tools assisted with programming, selected visual assets and some text. The game does not generate AI content during play.

Progress is saved locally in this browser. Clearing the game's site data removes that local progress.
