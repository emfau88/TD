PLAYER DATA / SERVICES
Local browser storage for progress, language and sound preferences.
No game-owned accounts, payments, ad systems, analytics or cloud-save service are integrated.
No generative AI service is called during play.
The Kongregate build loads the official client API and submits three saved values:
Highest Wave, Best Gold per Minute, Enemies Defeated.
All three statistics use Max, Display in leaderboards enabled, Server API only disabled.
Best Gold per Minute is the historical certified safe rate, rounded down to whole gold/minute.
Probe, temporary and feature-preview games do not submit scores.
This is not a cloud-save or cheat-proof backend service.
The host platform may apply its own services and data practices.

CREDITS
Creator names and license notices remain in-game. There are no clickable external links in the credits. Full source addresses and required license texts are plain text in the local notices and LICENSES.txt inside the ZIP.
